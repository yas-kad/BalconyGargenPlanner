# Low maintenance balcony plants

Low maintenance does not mean no water. It means a plant that looks fine if you only visit it once or twice a week.

  src="/images/blog-low-hero.jpg"

## Good first plants

- **Snake plant** handles shade and dry soil. It is a poor choice if you have pets, because the leaves are toxic.
- **Sedum and other stonecrops** store water in their leaves and suit a hot, exposed railing.
- **Rosemary and lavender** prefer to dry out between drinks, which suits a busy week.
- **Ornamental grasses** in a deep pot move in the wind and rarely need cutting back before spring.

## What actually saves time

Group pots so one watering can reaches all of them. A larger pot dries out more slowly than three tiny ones. Skip anything that needs daily misting or a greenhouse.

If you want flowers with almost no work, geraniums are still the easiest summer colour.
