# How to water balcony plants in summer

Pots on a balcony dry out faster than the same plant in the ground. In hot weather, most of them want a deep drink rather than a splash every few hours.

## A routine that works

1. Water in the morning, before the sun is on the leaves.
2. Push a finger into the soil. If the top few centimetres are dry, water until it runs out of the drainage hole.
3. Empty saucers after about half an hour. Roots left in water rot, even in summer.
4. On a heatwave, check again in the evening. Small pots and anything in full sun may need a second drink.

## Plants that want less

Rosemary, lavender, olive and succulents prefer to dry between waterings. If the leaves of a succulent go soft and translucent, it is more likely overwatered than thirsty.

A layer of gravel on top of the soil does not keep moisture in. A slightly larger pot, and watering the soil rather than the leaves, does.
