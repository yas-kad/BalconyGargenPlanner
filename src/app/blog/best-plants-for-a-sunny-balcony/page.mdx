# Best plants for a sunny balcony

A balcony that gets six hours of sun or more can grow a lot, as long as the pots can drain and you water before the soil turns to dust.

## Plants that enjoy the heat

- **Rosemary** keeps its shape in wind and only wants water when the top of the soil is dry.
- **Lavender** needs the sunniest corner and a pot that never sits in a saucer of water.
- **Geraniums** (pelargoniums) flower for months and forgive a missed day.
- **Olive**, in a large pot, is the one taller plant. Give it the heaviest container you can lift.
- **Thyme** fills the gaps along the front of a trough.
- **Bougainvillea** is worth it only if your winters stay mild. Elsewhere, treat it as a summer plant.

## One thing they all share

Use potting mix made for containers, not garden soil, and make sure every pot has a drainage hole. On a hot wall, a pale pot stays cooler than a black one.

Availability may vary, so check a local nursery before you go.
